# 🏎️ 24-Hour Endurance Racing Operational Manual
## Ajith Kumar Racing (AKR) Official Procedures

### 1. Driver Stint Regulations
- **Maximum Continuous Driving Time:** 3 hours per stint.
- **Minimum Rest Period:** 2 hours between rotations.
- **Hydration:** Mandatory 1.2L electrolytic hydration pack replenishment on driver swap.

### 2. Night Stint Visibility & Headlight Alignment
- High-intensity LED laser headlights calibrated to illuminate 300m apex braking marks.
- Yellow tint lens overlays utilized during fog or heavy atmospheric moisture.
