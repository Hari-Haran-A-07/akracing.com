# 💨 Aerodynamic Rake & Ground Effect Venturi Matrix

Understanding pitch-sensitive aerodynamic performance is vital for setup optimization at Monza, Spa, and Dubai.

## Downforce vs Front Ride Height

- **Front Ride Height 40mm - 45mm**: Peak underfloor venturi suction. Maximum ground effect efficiency.
- **Front Ride Height < 38mm**: High risk of front splitter stalling over curbs and bottoming out.
- **Rear Rake 60mm - 72mm**: Optimal diffuser expansion angle (~8.5°). Maximizes total aerodynamic downforce without detaching rear laminar airflow.
